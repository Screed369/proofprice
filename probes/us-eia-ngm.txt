<h4>September <em>Natural Gas Monthly</em>, containing data for July 2026</h4>
 
<ul>
<li>In July 2026, dry natural gas production increased year over year for the 16th straight month. Preliminary dry natural gas production in July 2026 was 3,525 billion cubic feet (Bcf), or 113.71 billion cubic feet per day (Bcf/d). Production was 5.3% (5.71 Bcf/d) higher in July 2026 than in July 2025 (108.00 Bcf/d). Daily dry natural gas production was the highest for any month since 1973, the earliest year in this dataset. Gross withdrawals also increased from July 2025:
	</li>
	
	<ul>
	<li><strong>Gross withdrawals:</strong> 4,247 Bcf for the month, or 137.00 Bcf/d</li>
	
		<ul>
		<li>A 5.6% increase compared with July 2025 (129.77 Bcf/d)</li>
		<li>Highest daily rate for any month since 1980, the earliest year in this dataset</li>
		</ul>	
	</ul>
	
	
<li>Estimated natural gas consumption in July 2026 was 2,776 Bcf, or 89.55 Bcf/d</li>

	<ul>
	<li>A 2.1% increase compared with July 2025 (87.74 Bcf/d)</li>
	<li>Highest daily rate for the month of July since 2001, the earliest year in this dataset</li>
	</ul>
	
<li>The year-over-year average daily rate of dry natural gas consumption in July 2026 increased in three of the four consuming sectors. Natural gas deliveries by consuming sector in July 2026 were:</li>
	
<ul>

	<li><strong>Residential deliveries:</strong> 110.8 Bcf for the month, or 3.57 Bcf/d</li>
		<ul>
		<li>A 0.3% decrease compared with July 2025 (3.58 Bcf/d)</li>
		</ul>
		
	<li><strong>Commercial deliveries:</strong> 154 Bcf for the month, or 4.97 Bcf/d</li>
		<ul>
		<li>A 0.6% increase compared with July 2025 (4.94 Bcf/d)</li>
		<li>Highest daily rate of commercial deliveries for the month of July since 1973, the earliest year in this dataset.</li>
		</ul>
		
	<li><strong>Industrial deliveries:</strong> 685 Bcf for the month, or 22.10 Bcf/d</li>
		<ul>
		<li>A 1.1% increase compared with July 2025 (21.87 Bcf/d)</li>
		<li>Second-highest daily rate of industrial deliveries for the month of July since 2001, the earliest year in this dataset.</li>
		</ul>
		
	<li><strong>Electric power deliveries:</strong> 1,531 Bcf for the month, or 49.39 Bcf/d</li>
		<ul>
		<li>A 2.5% increase compared with July 2025 (48.19 Bcf/d)</li>
    	<li>Highest daily rate of electric power deliveries for any month since 2001, the earliest year in this dataset.</li>
		
		</ul>
		
</ul>

	
<li>Net natural gas exports (exports minus imports) were 583 Bcf, or 18.81 Bcf/d, in July 2026. This net export daily volume was the highest for the month of July since we began tracking net trade in 1973. The United States exported over three times more natural gas than it imported in July 2026, as natural gas imports decreased while exports increased year over year:</li>
	
	<ul>
	<li><strong>Total natural gas imports: </strong> 244 Bcf for the month, or 7.87 Bcf/d</li>
		<ul>
		<li>A 5.4% decrease compared with July 2025 (8.32 Bcf/d)</li>
		<li>Lowest daily rate of natural gas imports for the month of July since 2021</li>
		</ul>
	<li><strong>Total natural gas exports: </strong> 827 Bcf for the month, or 26.68 Bcf/d</li>
		<ul>
		<li>A 14.9% increase compared with July 2025 (23.23 Bcf/d)</li>
		<li>Highest daily rate of natural gas exports for the month of July since we began tracking them in 1973</li>
		<li><strong>Liquefied natural gas (LNG) exports: </strong>   The daily rate of LNG exports in July 2026 was 18.7% higher than the daily rate in July 2025, the highest rate for the month of July since we began tracking LNG exports in 1997. In July 2026, the United States exported 16.74 Bcf/d of LNG to 36 countries.</li>
		
		</ul>
	</ul>

<br />
<strong>Note: </strong> Derived numbers may differ from those reported in the tables due to independent rounding.
