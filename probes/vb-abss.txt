                        <h5 class="sm">Description</h5>
                        <div>
                            <p>This database provides information on sole-source contracts of $10,000 or more for services purchased by Alberta government departments. It includes sole-source contracts for government business  and for direct human services and supports.  The database is updated quarterly.</p>
