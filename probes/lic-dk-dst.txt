<h1>Source references and use of our logo</h1>
<!-- End: Header -->

<div class="cludoContent">

<!-- Begin: TextContent -->
    <p class="lead">You are free to re-use and reproduce contents from dst.dk and statbank.dk. This includes even commercial use. You should, however, always include a source accreditation to Statistics Denmark. You are, however, not allowed to use our logo.</p>
<h2>Source references</h2>
<p>The source reference should link to the StatBank or www.dst.dk, where the user can see the data in context.</p>
<p><strong>Examples of correct source reference:</strong></p>
<p><strong>Dst.dk:</strong></p>
<p>Source: Statistics Denmark - https://www.dst.dk/en/Statistik/emner/oekonomi/offentlig-oekonomi/sociale-udgifter</p>
<p><strong>StatBank.dk:</strong></p>
<p>Source: Statistics Denmark &ndash; StatBank.dk/folk1a (the code following StatBank.dk/ indicates the specific table from which the figures are extracted.)</p>
<p>You are welcome to reproduce our graphic figures or make your own graphic figures on the basis of our data. Statistics Denmark must also in this context be cited as source.</p>
<p>If you subject our data to further processing; this must be clearly stated. This can be done, e.g. by writing: &rdquo;Source&rdquo; Own calculations based on data from Statistics Denmark."</p>
<h2>Copying and use of content</h2>
<p>The content on dst.dk and statistikbanken.dk can be freely copied and used - except for photos. An agreement with Statistics Denmark is required here - contact <a href="javascript:mSend('presse')">presse@dst.dk</a>.</p>
<p>Press photos of Statistics Denmark's executive board may, however, be reproduced freely.</p>
<p>Videos can only be reused in their unaltered form.</p>
<h2>Use of Statistics Denmark's logo</h2>
<p>Statistics Denmark's logo may only be used by others than Statistics Denmark when it comes to collaborative projects such as publications where the use of the logo has been agreed.</p>
<h2>Open data</h2>
<p>&nbsp;Statistics Denmark's open data are official statistics data, which are electronically available in a way that users can apply them directly in various applications. They can be accessed either via a user interface or via an <a href="/en/Statistik/brug-statistikken/muligheder-i-statistikbanken/api" id="link_1524580733141">API </a>against the StatBank. Data can be freely used in the development of various services, including development of apps for smartphones.</p>
