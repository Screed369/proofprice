    <h1>
      
        Health insurance premiums
        
        
      
    </h1>
    
      
        <div class="notes embedded-content">
          <p>The Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH) is the authority for the supervision of the insurers who offer social health insurance i.e., the mandatory health care insurance (basic insurance) and the voluntary daily allowance insurance. Starting January 1st 2016 the activity of surveillance is regulated by the new Health Insurance Surveillance Act. The FOPH is responsible for insurers applying  the Health Insurance Act unilaterally. The premiums of the insurers are examined and approved by the FOPH. Prior FOPH’s approval the premiums can neither be published nor implemented.</p>
        </div>
