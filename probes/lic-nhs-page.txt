    <h1>
      
        Prescription Cost Analysis (PCA) Monthly Administrative Data
        
        
      
    </h1>
    
    <div class="notes embedded-content">
      <h2>Dataset Description</h2>
<p>Prescription Cost Analysis (PCA) shows the total volume and cost for drugs, dressings, appliances, and medical devices that have been dispensed in the community in England and submitted to the NHSBSA for reimbursement monthly.&nbsp;</p>
<p>This dataset is designed to provide the total number of prescription items and spend for any individual product, at any level of the British National Formulary (BNF) hierarchy. For example, for Paracetamol 500mg tablets, or collections of products such as antibacterial drugs, antidepressant drugs, or dressings.&nbsp;</p>
<p>This data mirrors the methodology used in the PCA Annual National Statistics publication.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<p>The latest statistical release, along with more information on what PCA contains, can be found on the <a href="https://www.nhsbsa.nhs.uk/statistical-collections/prescription-cost-analysis-england">NHSBSA website</a> and you can specifically read the <a href="https://nhsbsa-opendata.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/pca/pca_background_info_methodology_v001.html">Prescription Cost Analysis Background Information and Methodology</a>. The monthly PCA release is a monthly management information data set and is not an Official Statistic.&nbsp;</p>
<p style="margin: 0;">Data for February 2008 to March 2021 is available on the <a href="https://www.nhsbsa.nhs.uk/prescription-data/dispensing-data/prescription-cost-analysis-pca-data">NHSBSA website</a> in a legacy format.&nbsp;</p>
<h2 style="margin: 0 0 5px 0;">Overview of Service</h2>
<p>
<p style="margin: 0;">Information about our NHSBSA Prescriptions Data service can be found here - <a href="https://www.nhsbsa.nhs.uk/prescription-data">Prescription data | NHSBSA</a></p>

<h2>Caveats</h2>
<div class="nhsuk-inset-text">
  <span class="nhsuk-u-visually-hidden">Information: </span>
  <h3 class="nhsuk-u-font-size-32">Excluded Items</h3>
  The dataset does not include items that were not dispensed, were disallowed, or were returned to the contractor for further clarification.
</div>

<div class="nhsuk-inset-text">
  <span class="nhsuk-u-visually-hidden">Information: </span>
  <h3 class="nhsuk-u-font-size-32">Prescribing Contexts Not Included</h3>
  Prescriptions that are prescribed and dispensed within prisons, hospitals, or issued as private prescriptions are not included in this dataset.
</div>

<div class="nhsuk-inset-text">
  <span class="nhsuk-u-visually-hidden">Information: </span>
 <h3 class="nhsuk-u-font-size-32">Unsubmitted or Unclaimed Items</h3>
  Items that were prescribed but either not presented for dispensing, or not submitted to NHS Prescription Services by the dispenser, are also excluded.
</div>
