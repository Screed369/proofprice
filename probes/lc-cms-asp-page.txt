      <h1 class="page-title">
        <span class="field field--name-title field--type-string field--label-visually_hidden">Medicare Part B Drug Payment Limit File </span>

      </h1>
    </div>
  </div>




  <div data-block-plugin-id="inline_block:alert_block" class="block block-layout-builder block-inline-blockalert-block">
    
          

    <ds-alert >
      <span slot="heading">
                         <div class="ds-c-alert__heading field field--name-field-alert-heading field--type-string field--label-hidden field__item">Announcement</div>
           
      </span>
                       <div class="cms-text--prominent field field--name-field-alert-body field--type-text-long field--label-hidden field__item"><p><span>CMS may not publish a payment limit or crosswalk for all drugs that are reported by manufacturers. The absence or presence of a HCPCS, NDC code, and/or the payment limits in the Medicare Part B Payment Limit File does not indicate whether Medicare covers a particular product. Even if a product does not appear on a quarter’s Payment Limit File, the local Medicare Administrative Contractor (MAC) may process the Part B claim after determining the payment limit, provided that the claim is reasonable and necessary and meets all necessary requirements for payment. For additional information about the payment limit for new drugs and biological products during the initial period of marketing, refer to regulations at&nbsp;</span><a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-414/subpart-K#p-414.904(e)(4)"><span>42 CFR § 414.904(e)(4)</span></a><span> and rulemaking in the&nbsp;</span><a href="https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2018/11/23/2018-24170/medicare-program-revisions-to-payment-policies-under-the-physician-fee-schedule-and-other-revisions"><span>CY 2019 PFS final rule</span></a><span> (83 FR 59661 through 59666) and the&nbsp;</span><a href="https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/11/16/2023-24184/medicare-and-medicaid-programs-cy-2024-payment-policies-under-the-physician-fee-schedule-and-other"><span>CY 2024 PFS final rule</span></a><span> (88 FR 79040 through 79042).</span></p>
</div>
           
    </ds-alert>

        
  </div>
<div data-block-plugin-id="inline_block:basic" class="block block-layout-builder block-inline--basic block-inline--cms-block-text">
  
    
      <div class="content">
                       <div class="cms-text--prominent field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><p><span>The quarterly Medicare Part B Drug Payment Limit Files are grouped by the year in which they are effective. For more information on how ASP is reported, and what drugs and biologicals are included in the payment limit files, visit </span><a href="https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/part-b-drugs/asp-reporting"><span>ASP Reporting</span></a><span>.&nbsp;</span></p></div>
