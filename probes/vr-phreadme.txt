Notes:

- The publication date is around the 23rd of the month prior to it becoming effective.

- Documentation is included in the `Schedule XML schema <http://schedule.pharmac.govt.nz/2006/07/Schedule.xsd>`_.

- The printed Schedule is produced from the XML file using XSLT and TeX, so the XML files are preferred over the MSAccess databases contained in the zip files.  The MSAccess files are necessary for some legacy systems, but their publication will be discontinued as soon as is practicable.

- Other than the pack id (Pharmacode), all entity identifiers are generated by PHARMAC and do not relate to any other terminology. They can occasionally change without notice.

- Although technical support is only provided for stakeholders in the New Zealand pharmaceutical claiming network, PHARMAC staff will endeavour to answer occasional queries sent to `texschedule@pharmac.govt.nz`_.
