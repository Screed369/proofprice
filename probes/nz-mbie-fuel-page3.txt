                <h1 class="content-page-heading">
                    Weekly fuel price monitoring
                    
                </h1>

                
                    <p class="page-intro">We carry out weekly monitoring of national average prices and retail importer margins for regular petrol, premium petrol, and diesel. This data is updated weekly, using the previous week&#039;s data.</p>
                

                
    <div class="jumplink-content">
        <h2 data-ignore>
            <span class="title">On this page</span>
            <span class="tereotitle" lang="mi">I tēnei whārangi</span>
        </h2>
        <div id="jumplinks" class="jumplinks"></div>
    </div>

	   <div class="element mbie__elements__boxelement" id="e82807">
	
    <div class="box-element">
        
        <p><strong>Fuel response support</strong><br>Information about the price the government uses to inform temporary fuel response support can be found on our Price for fuel response support page.</p>
<p><a href="/building-and-energy/energy-and-natural-resources/energy-statistics-and-modelling/energy-statistics/weekly-fuel-price-monitoring/price-for-fuel-response-support">Price for fuel response support</a></p>
<p><strong>Update on Wednesday 7 October 2026</strong><br>MBIE has updated its weekly fuel price monitoring methodology to reflect additional costs that fuel importers have faced due to the 2026 Middle East conflict.</p>
<p>The Commerce Commission, in consultation with fuel importers, has identified additional costs associated with importing and supplying fuel. MBIE has worked with the Commerce Commission to incorporate these costs in our weekly fuel price data.</p>
<p>To better reflect current market conditions and account for these additional costs, we have applied a fuel market adjustment to all fuel types from 27 February 2026. This has resulted in revisions to the importer cost, importer margin, and importer margin trend series.</p>
<p>More information on these changes can be found on our weekly fuel price monitoring methodology document.</p>
<p><a href="/assets/weekly-fuel-price-monitoring-methodology.pdf">Weekly fuel price monitoring methodology<span class="fileExt"> [PDF, 187 KB]</span></a></p>
    </div>

	<div class="content-element">
    
        <h2 class="elemental-heading">
            Purpose
            
        </h2>
    
    <p>Our monitoring shows how recent movements in key factors, such as international oil prices, are affecting retail pump prices that consumers are facing in New Zealand.</p>
<p>This monitoring was a recommendation of the 2008 New Zealand Petrol Review to promote transparency in retail petrol and diesel pricing.&nbsp;</p>
<p><a href="/building-and-energy/energy-and-natural-resources/energy-statistics-and-modelling/energy-statistics/weekly-fuel-price-monitoring#reviewnzpm">Review of the New Zealand petrol market</a></p>
</div>
