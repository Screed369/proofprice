        <div class="govuk-grid-column-two-thirds">
            <p class="gem-c-lead-paragraph">
    How to report fuel prices.
</p>
        </div>

      <div class="govuk-grid-column-two-thirds">
          <div data-module="govspeak" class="gem-c-govspeak govuk-!-margin-bottom-0">
    
<div role="note" aria-label="Information" class="application-notice info-notice">
  <p>From 2 February 2026, you must submit fuel price updates within 30 minutes of any change.</p>
</div>

<p>There are 4 ways that you can use to update your fuel prices.</p>


</div>


              <div id="default-id-52ca56e6" data-module="govuk-accordion gem-accordion ga4-event-tracker" data-ga4-expandable="" data-anchor-navigation="true" data-show-text="Show" data-hide-text="Hide" data-show-all-text="Show all sections" data-hide-all-text="Hide all sections" data-this-section-visually-hidden=" this section" class="gem-c-accordion govuk-accordion govuk-!-margin-bottom-6">
      <div class="govuk-accordion__section">
        <div class="govuk-accordion__section-header">
          <h2 class="govuk-accordion__section-heading" id="updating-prices-through-the-service" data-ga4-event='{"event_name":"select_content","type":"accordion","text":"Updating prices through the service","index_section":1,"index_section_count":4}'><span class="govuk-accordion__section-button" id="default-id-52ca56e6-heading-1">Updating prices through the service</span></h2>
          
</div>        <div id="default-id-52ca56e6-content-1" class="govuk-accordion__section-content" data-module="ga4-link-tracker" data-ga4-set-indexes="" data-ga4-link='{"event_name":"navigation","type":"accordion","section":"Updating prices through the service","index_section":1,"index_section_count":4}'><div data-module="govspeak" class="gem-c-govspeak govuk-!-margin-bottom-0">
    <p>Any assigned user can update fuel prices, as long as they’re associated with the forecourt.</p><ol class="steps">
  <li>
    <p>Go to the ‘Forecourts’ page and select the relevant forecourt.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>Select ‘Update fuel prices’.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>You will be prompted to select the fuel grades that you want to update the prices for. Select each grade being updated and click ‘Continue’.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>Enter the new price for each grade that you selected. The price must be entered in pence per litre (<abbr title="pence per litre">ppl</abbr>). The number can include up to 3 decimal places.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>Enter the date and time that the price changed. If you’re reporting the change more than 30 minutes after the price change, you can choose to give a reason for the late submission.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>Click ‘Continue’.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>The system will validate your fuel prices by comparing them with the previously submitted values. It will warn you if the values are invalid or outside expected parameters (for example, if they’re more than 50% of the previous price). You can then choose to change the price or ignore the warnings.</p>
  </li>
</ol>
</div></div>
</div>      <div class="govuk-accordion__section">
        <div class="govuk-accordion__section-header">
          <h2 class="govuk-accordion__section-heading" id="updating-prices-through-the-api" data-ga4-event='{"event_name":"select_content","type":"accordion","text":"Updating prices through the API\n","index_section":2,"index_section_count":4}'><span class="govuk-accordion__section-button" id="default-id-52ca56e6-heading-2">Updating prices through the API
</span></h2>
          
</div>        <div id="default-id-52ca56e6-content-2" class="govuk-accordion__section-content" data-module="ga4-link-tracker" data-ga4-set-indexes="" data-ga4-link='{"event_name":"navigation","type":"accordion","section":"Updating prices through the API\n","index_section":2,"index_section_count":4}'><div data-module="govspeak" class="gem-c-govspeak govuk-!-margin-bottom-0">
    <p>You can use the public <abbr title="Application Programming Interface">API</abbr> to update your fuel prices by integrating the data into your own tools and services.</p><p>You’ll need:</p><ul>
  <li>a motor fuel trader registered GOV.UK One Login to access the <abbr title="Application Programming Interface">API</abbr>
</li>
