<h3 id="introduction">Introduction</h3>
<p>The Data Export from FOPH is based on the IDMP standard added with several datafields for the Spezialitätenliste.</p>

<h3 id="data-pipeline">Data Pipeline</h3>
<p>The following datapipeline overview illustrates the dataflow of medicinal product data in the Swiss health landscape. The focus of this FHIR-IG is the datacommunication between FOPH (3) and Health Insurance Companies (9).</p>

<p>The red marked lines indicate all communications planned to be moved to FHIR within the next 5 - 10 years. This includes the following Organisations and steps:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Marketing Authorisation Holder</li>
  <li>Swissmedic (Medicines Regulatory Agency, Switzerland)</li>
  <li>Federal Office of Public Health</li>
  <li>RefData Foundation</li>
  <li>Value-added Data Provider</li>
  <li>Medical Software Provider</li>
  <li>Helathcare Provider (Hospitals, Medical Office, Pharmacies, Residential Home)</li>
  <li>Care Receiver (Patient, Care giver)</li>
  <li>Health Insurance Companies</li>
</ol>

<p>FOPH is publishing the information on reimbursed products via the Spezialitätenliste website ((3) to www) and via FHIR ((3) - (9)).</p>

<p><i>Fig. 1: Data Pipeline</i></p>

<h3 id="epl-datamodel-of-the-spezialitätenliste-incl-the-dataelements-used-by-foph">ePL Datamodel of the Spezialitätenliste incl. the dataelements used by FOPH</h3>
<p>The following datamodel illustrates the addapted IDMP datamodel based on ISO 11615:2017 addapted for EPL of the Federal Office for Public Health FOPH.</p>

<p>IDMP classes and datalelements not used by FOPH have been removed from the basic model and ePL classes and elements have been added.</p>

<p><img src="EPL-Datamodel-Medicinal_Product.png" class="figure-img img-responsive img-rounded center-block" alt="Fig. 2: EPL Datamodel - Authorised Medicinal Products plus Spezialitätenliste" style="width:" /></p>
<div style="clear:both;"></div>
<p><i>Fig. 2: EPL Datamodel - Authorised Medicinal Products plus Spezialitätenliste</i></p>

<p>The dataelement extensions defined by EMA and Swissmedic are marked with [ext], while the extendions defined by FOPH are marked with [foph].</p>

<h3 id="datamodel-for-the-full-authorised-medicinal-products-plus-spezialitätenliste">Datamodel for the full Authorised Medicinal Products plus Spezialitätenliste</h3>
<p>This chapter has been removed, as the datamodel published by Swissmedic is under development. This chapter will be added, as soon as Swissmedic is publishing the full model.</p>

<h3 id="fhir-bundle">FHIR Bundle</h3>
<p>This exchange format is defined as a FHIR Bundle resource of type collection containing a list of IDMP resource entries.</p>
