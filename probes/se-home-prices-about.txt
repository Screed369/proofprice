<h1 id='h1'>Real estate prices and registrations of title</h1>

    <div>
        <img class="sos-logotype-large sos-logotype-large-mb" src="/img/SOS-icons/SOS_svart_En_3.svg" title="Belongs to Official Statistics of Sweden" alt="Official statistics of Sweden" />
    </div>

<div class="row topIntro">

        <p>
            <a href="/en/finding-statistics/publishing-calendar/?prodKod=BO0501&period=Forward1Year" class="link-large link-icon-calendar link-icon">
                <span>Upcoming  publishing: 2026-10-23</span>
            </a>
        </p>
    <p class="clearfix ingress">The statistics present price level, price trends and turnover in the real estate market for granted registrations of title via registration of title deeds or registration of site leaseholds. The statistics covers the entire real estate market. The survey also presents comprehensive turnover statistics of tenant-owned dwellings.</p>

</div>
