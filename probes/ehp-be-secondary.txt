    <h1 id="main_page_title" class="page-header">
        Sales on the secondary market 
  </h1>

<section class="block block--ctools block--ctools--node">
  
    

        
<article class="node node--type-theme-subject node--view-mode-full ds-1col clearfix">

  

  



            <div class="field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><p><span class="text-info">Statbel is publishing statistics on real estate sales on the secondary market (existing dwellings). These statistics are produced as part of the calculation of the house price index and are provided to Eurostat.</span></p></div>
      <div class=" field-group-tabs-wrapper"><div data-vertical-tabs-panes><input class="vertical-tabs__active-tab" type="hidden" /><div class="js-form-wrapper form-wrapper form-item js-form-item panel panel-default" id="news">

              </div>
      
            
    
    <div id="news--content" class="panel-body panel-collapse collapse fade in">
            
            <div class="field field--name-field-news field--type-entity-reference field--label-hidden field--item">  
<article class="node node--type-news node--view-mode-embed ds-1col clearfix">

  

  
            <div class="field field--name-node-title field--type-ds field--label-hidden field--item"><h1>
  Real estate sales – 2nd quarter 2026
</h1>
</div>
      
            <div class="field field--name-field-theme field--type-entity-reference field--label-hidden field--item">Housing</div>
      
            <div class="field field--name-field-publication-date field--type-datetime field--label-hidden field--item"><time datetime="2026-09-25T12:00:00Z">25 September 2026</time>
</div>
      



            <div class="field field--name-field-image field--type-image field--label-hidden field--item">  <img loading="lazy" src="/sites/default/files/styles/news_full/public/images/Bouwen%20%26%20wonen/2.7%20vastgoedprijsindex/AdobeStock_238339456.jpeg?itok=5C7vs4Cw" width="430" height="287" alt="Real estate sales – 2nd quarter 2026" class="img-responsive" />


</div>
      
            <div class="field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><ul>
	<li>The number of real estate transactions went down by 11.0% in the second quarter 2026 compared to the same quarter the year before.</li>
	<li>The value of real estate transactions went down by 7.6% in the second quarter 2026 compared to the same quarter the year before.</li>
	<li>Compared to the first quarter 2026, the number of transactions has decreased by 4.5% and the value of transactions by 6.7%.</li>
</ul>

<p>In the second quarter 2026,<strong> the number of real estate transactions decreased by 4.5% compared to the first quarter 2026 and the value of transactions by 6.7%. The index of the number of transactions</strong> stands at 87.62 points (2025=100) versus 91.77 points in the first quarter 2026. <strong>The index of the value of transactions</strong> stands at 88.31 points (2025=100) versus 94.65 points.</p>

<div data-embed-button="special_content" data-entity-embed-display="view_mode:media.embed_entity" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="8479d723-7bcb-4858-8da5-4e41928f2b32" data-langcode="en" data-entity-embed-display-settings="[]" class="embedded-entity"><div>
  
  
            <div class="field field--name-field-body field--type-text-long field--label-hidden field--item"><div style="position: relative; width: 100%; height: 0px; padding: 62.58% 0px 0px; overflow: hidden; will-change: transform;"><iframe loading="lazy" src="https://e.infogram.com/53ecdc69-adf6-4a0d-bc55-ab7d18ed4006?src=embed&amp;embed_type=responsive_iframe" title="SecHs 2026-Q2 G1 EN" allowfullscreen allow="fullscreen" style="position: absolute; width: 100%; height: 100%; top: 0px; left: 0px; border-width: medium; border-style: none; border-color: currentcolor; border-image: none; padding: 0px; margin: 0px;"></iframe></div></div>
      
</div>
</div>


<p>In the second quarter 2026, the number of transactions decreased by 5.7% in Flanders and by 3.5% in Wallonia but increased by 1.3% in Brussels compared to the first quarter 2026.</p>

<div data-embed-button="special_content" data-entity-embed-display="view_mode:media.embed_entity" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="8ba3e8ed-8d46-4cde-a543-3f1c34a91e0f" data-langcode="en" data-entity-embed-display-settings="[]" class="embedded-entity"><div>
  
  
            <div class="field field--name-field-body field--type-text-long field--label-hidden field--item"><div style="position: relative; width: 100%; height: 0px; padding: 62.58% 0px 0px; overflow: hidden; will-change: transform;"><iframe loading="lazy" src="https://e.infogram.com/d827c4e6-553c-49ba-9d7a-2b10cde74da0?src=embed&amp;embed_type=responsive_iframe" title="SecHs 2026-Q2 G2 EN" allowfullscreen allow="fullscreen" style="position: absolute; width: 100%; height: 100%; top: 0px; left: 0px; border-width: medium; border-style: none; border-color: currentcolor; border-image: none; padding: 0px; margin: 0px;"></iframe></div></div>
      
</div>
</div>


<p>In the second quarter 2026, the value of transactions decreased by 5.2% in Flanders, by 5.8% in Wallonia and by 18.2% in Brussels compared to the first quarter 2026.</p>

<p>These indices are based on real estate transaction data from the General Administration of the Patrimonial Documentation of the FPS Finances. The results for the latest quarters are considered provisional and may be reviewed during the following quarter when additional data become available. These figures relate to sales on the secondary market (existing dwellings) and therefore exclude new dwellings.</p>

      <div class="field field--name-field-document-description field--type-text-long field--label-hidden field__item"><h2><strong>Purpose and short description</strong></h2>

<p>The analysis and monitoring of housing markets require high-quality official statistics for the national, European Union and euro area markets. Information on the evolution of house prices is particularly important for financial stability and macro-prudential supervision and for economic and monetary policy making.</p>

<h2><strong>Survey population</strong></h2>

<p>Real estate transactions involving residential properties. These statistics only cover the secondary market (sales of existing dwellings).</p>

<h2><strong>Periodicity</strong></h2>

<p>Quarterly.</p>

<h2><strong>Release calendar</strong></h2>

<p>Results available 3 months after the reference period. The data for the last quarter are considered provisional and will be revised in the next publication.</p>

<h2><strong>Definitions</strong></h2>

<p><strong>House sales</strong>: measure the number of dwellings sold (volume/value). These data are useful for analysing activity and liquidity in the housing market.</p>

<h2><strong>Technical information</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Number of transactions:</strong></p>

<p>Expressed as an index (2025 = 100), in absolute values and as an annual rate of change</p>

<p><strong>Value of transactions:</strong></p>

<p>Expressed as an index (2025 = 100), in absolute values (in euros) and as an annual rate of change</p></div>
