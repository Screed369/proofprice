|Bill|Total Hospital Bill associated with the episode of care.
||Includes A&E charges if any.
||Excludes other charges which are not associated to this episode of care eg. antenatal packages, outpatient treatments etc.
|Operation Fee|Total charge associated with the TOSP procedure, i.e. a sum of any surgeon, anaesthetist, and facility fees.
|Surgeon Fee|Professional fee of surgeon for performing the TOSP procedure.
||Includes the total sum of surgeon fees (except anaesthetist), if more than one surgeon is involved.
|Anaesthetist Fee|Professional fee of anaesthetist for the TOSP procedure.
|Facility Fee|Non-professional charges for the use of the operating facility (eg. operating theatre, medical consumables used during the operation, assistant nurses provided by the hospital etc).
|Implant Fee|Total charges for the implant(s) used in the TOSP procedure.
|Other Fee|Remaining charges after accounting for Total Operation Fee and Total Implant Amount.
|Room Fee|Total charges associated with ward fees, general nursing services during admission and non-medical amenities provided as part of the ward.
|Drug Fee|Total charges for all medication and drug fees, including fees for the preparation and administration of medications and drugs.
|Investigation Fee|Total charges for investigations of materials derived from the body for the purpose of diagnosis, prevention or treatment of disease (e.g. histology, biochemisty, cytology)
|Doctors' Attendance Fee|Doctor attendance fee. Covers professional consultation and/or specialist attendance fee. Excludes any professional fee charged either under the Operations grouping or Room and Board grouping.
