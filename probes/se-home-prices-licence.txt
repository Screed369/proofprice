        <h1>
            Open data
        </h1>
        <div class="ingress">

            Statistics Sweden&#x2019;s open data is available for everyone to use free of charge.
        </div>
        <div class="xhtmlText">
            
<h2>Open data offered by Statistics Sweden</h2>
<h3><a href="/en/services/open-data-api/pxwebapi/api-for-the-statistical-database/" target="_top">The Statistical Database</a></h3>
<p>The Statistical Database contains official statistics from Statistics Sweden and other statistical agencies.</p>
<h3><a href="/en/services/open-data-api/key-ratios-by-industry-and-size-class/" target="_top">Industry business ratios</a></h3>
<p>Industry business ratios compare a company’s strong and weak sides using key ratios for various industries.&nbsp;</p>
<h3><a href="/en/services/open-data-api/open-geodata/" target="_top">Geodata</a></h3>
<p>Geodata describes everything that has a geographic location, such as localities, activity units or grid statistics.</p>
<h3><a href="/vara-tjanster/oppna-data/myndighetsregistret/" target="_top">Government Agencies Register</a></h3>
<p>The Government Agencies Register is a register of government agencies in Sweden.</p>
<h3><a href="/vara-tjanster/oppna-data/vardefulla-datamangder-hvd/">High-Value Datasets</a></h3>
<p>High-Value Datasets are information that EU has classified as particularly valuable and of high value for public benefit and innovation.</p>
<h2>How you may use Statistics Sweden's open data</h2>
<p><a href="/en/services/open-data-api/">Statistics Sweden’s statistics are accessible via API (scb.se)</a></p>
<p>You may use Statistics Sweden’s open data free of charge.&nbsp;We use the licence CC0 for this data, which means that you may use and disseminate or provide such data without any requirement to state the source.</p>
<p>If you choose to state the source, we recommend putting (when stating it in English): “Source: Statistics Sweden”.</p>
<p>Statistics Sweden’s database also includes statistics from other government agencies. If you use statistics made available over Statistics Sweden’s API, which have a source reference to another agency, you should state this.</p>
<p>If you yourself have processed data from Statistics Sweden, do not state the source.</p>
<p>You may not use the API to spread malicious code.</p>
<p>You may not present a service as an “official cooperation” or “partnership” with Statistics Sweden.</p>
<h2>How Statistics Sweden’s API works</h2>
<p>Statistics Sweden's API is provided as it is. You may perform a maximum of ten call-ups in a 10-second period from an IP address. You yourself choose how the questions are distributed. For example, you can choose to ask ten questions in the first second and then be denied the nine following times.&nbsp;You can retrieve a maximum of 100,000 values per table. The error message “HTTP 403 Forbidden” indicates that the maximum value has been exceeded.</p>
<h2>How corrections of the statistics are handled</h2>
<p>The statistics distributed by Statistics Sweden via the API are continually updated as we correct source data.&nbsp;We only communicate the correction in the Statistical Database.</p>
<h2>Open data directives</h2>
<p>On its digital agenda, the Government has an ambition to improve the conditions for further use of public information from government agencies. It is also an aspect that is regulated in the Inspire Directive from the EU.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.geodata.se/styrande/inspire/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The EU INSPIRE Directive</a></p>
