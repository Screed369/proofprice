/></div></div></div><div class="field field-name-body field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden"><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even"><p class="rtejustify">The Medicine Prices Monitoring Survey in Malaysia/ <i>Kajian Pemantauan Harga Ubat (KPHU)</i> is an initiative under the Pharmaceutical Services Programme (PSP) to monitor medicine prices in Malaysia. This study was conducted every two (2) years  to collect data related to the availability, affordability of medicines, and price-related components such as price mark-up information in Malaysia. The medicine prices monitoring was in line with the objectives of the Malaysian National Medicines Policy (MNMP) to improve the health level of Malaysians by promoting equitable access to essential medicines, rational use of medicines, and ensuring that medicines are of high quality, safe, effective, and affordable. This study was developed based on guidelines by the World Health Organisation (WHO) and Health Action International (HAI), which involve three main basket of medicines namely: WHO/HAI Basket, Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) Basket, and Single Product Registration Holder (PRH) Basket.</p></div></div></div><div class="field field-name-field-kategori field-type-list-text field-label-inline clearfix"><div class="field-label">Document categories:&nbsp;</div><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even">Publications</div></div></div><div class="field field-name-field-upload-files field-type-file field-label-above"><div class="field-label">Download files:&nbsp;</div><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even"><table class="sticky-enabled"><thead><tr><th>Attachment</th><th>Size</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr class="odd"><td><span class="file"><img class="file-icon" alt="PDF icon" title="application/pdf" src="/modules/file/icons/application-pdf.png" /> <a href="https://pharmacy.moh.gov.my/sites/default/files/document-upload/kajian-pemantauan-harga-ubat-2022_0.pdf" type="application/pdf;
