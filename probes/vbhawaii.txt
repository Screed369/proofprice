				<p>This section presents statistics on visitor arrivals, daily visitor census, visitor accommodation units, hotel occupancy rates, and average room rates.  The data in this section are complied from reports issued by the Hawaii State Department of Business, Economic Development &amp; Tourism (DBEDT), specifically the Tourism Research Branch of the Research &amp; Economic Analysis Division (READ).  Additional data sources include the Hawaii Tourism Authority (HTA), the Hawaii Visitors Bureau (HVB), the Hawaii Visitors &amp; Convention Bureau (HVCB), PKF-Hawaii, Smith Travel Research, and Hospitality Advisors, LLC.</p>
<p>For historical data and long-term visitor trends, readers may refer to Sections 11 and 20 of <em>Historical Statistics of Hawaii</em>.</p>
