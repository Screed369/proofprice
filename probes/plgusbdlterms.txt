                        <div class="card-body fontSizeCard">
                            <p>
                                LOCAL DATA BANK (POL.: BDL) is Poland's largest database of the economy, society and the environment.
                            </p>
                            <p>
                                BDL offers thousands statistical data grouped thematically. The first data comes from 1995.
                            </p>
                            <p>
                                <ul>
                                    <li>Data and indicators describe statistical towns, municipalities, counties, voivodships and Poland as well as units compliant with the NUTS nomenclature: subregions, regions and macroregions.</li>
                                    <li>Information resources (annual and short-term statistics) are constantly supplemented and updated.</li>
                                </ul>
                            </p>
                            <p>
                                The API application allows to browse and download for further processing the full range of data located in the Bank. The data is made available through REST API in XML and JSON format.
                            </p>
                            <p>
                                The BDL API was implemented as part of a project <a href="https://bip.stat.gov.pl/dzialalnosc-statystyki-publicznej/projekty-unijne-w-statystyce/otwarte-dane-dostep-standard-edukacja/">"Open data - access, standard, education"</a>, whose leader is the Ministry of Digitization. The project is co-financed from the European Union funds from the Operational Program Digital Poland.
                            </p>

                            <h2 class="card-title">Resource structure</h2>
                            <p>
                                The following BDL resources are made available in the API application:
                            </p>
                            <ol>
                                <li>
                                    Aggregates - data aggregation levels for which data are available, e.g. municipalities or urban-rural communes.
                                </li>
                                <li>
                                    Attributes - descriptions of specific situations in the data that are associated with each numerical value, e.g. 'Value smaller than the adopted presentation format'.    
                                </li>
                                <li>
                                    Data - numerical data in the form of three [real number, attribute identifier, year identifier], in the form of a data set for a specific variable or set of data for one unit (more information below).
                                </li>
                                <li>
                                    Units of measurement - units of measurement appearing in the data, related to specific variables, e.g. 'thousand liters'.
                                </li>
                                <li>
                                    Level of validity - the level of validity of data by:
                                    <ul>
                                        <li>0 - Polish level</li>
                                        <li>1 - Macro-regions level</li>
                                        <li>2 - Voivedships level</li>
                                        <li>3 - Regions level</li>
                                        <li>4 - Subregions level</li>
                                        <li>5 - Counties level</li>
                                        <li>6 - Municipalities level</li>
                                        <li>7 - Statistical towns level</li>
                                    </ul>
                                </li>
                                <li>
                                    Topics – hierarchically related groups of variables according to the substantive scope, eg. 'Population', 'Population by age and gender groups' (more information below).
                                </li>
                                <li>
                                    Units - hierarchically related list of territorial units (from Poland up to municipalities) and statistical towns.
                                </li>
                                <li>
                                    Variables - these are multidimensional features representing a specific phenomenon, with a defined validity in years and at a specific level of units, e.g. the number of employees for the ages of '20 -26 'and gender' men '.
                                </li>
                                <li>
                                    Years - years of validity for which data may exist, e.g. '2003', '2004'.
                                </li>
                            </ol>
                            <hr>
                            <h2 class="card-title">Variability and quantity of resources</h2>
                            <p>
                                Resources such as Aggregates, Attributes, Units of Measure and Years are dictionary resources with very low variability and multiplicity, therefore they can be treated as so-called 'typical dictionaries', occasionally updated at specific time intervals or at the user request. These resources are not paged and can’t be searched according to complicated criteria.
                            </p>
                            <p>
                                Resource such as Units, denoting the territorial division units, is a resource of large multiplicity and very small variability, updated only when inputting the data for the new year in BDL (at the beginning of the year). Due to its multiplicity, it may cause difficulties in downloading it in full size, therefore we are able to search and download partially according to our own criteria, of course in a limited scope.
                            </p>
                            <p>
                                Resources such as Data and Variables are resources with very high variability, because the Bank is constantly undergoing the process of extending the scope of data as well as verification and corrections.
                            </p>

                                The data is made available by the API in two alternative ways:

                                <li>
