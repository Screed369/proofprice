	<h2 id="Background" style=""><span id="Dataandinformationuseandattributionpolicy-Background"></span><strong>Background</strong>
 <i18n-message i18nkey="clipboard.copyLink.label" attribute="description">
  <copy-clipboard class="anchor-copy-button" value="https://help.fews.net/fdp/data-and-information-use-and-attribution-policy#Background">
   <svg role="img" aria-hidden="true" data-component="icon" class="icon icon-link copy-headline-link-icon">
    <use href="/__theme/b_8ca5a7d7-80f3-41c8-bea7-75cce14ad14f/scroll-sites-help-center-theme/57/icons.svg#link"></use>
   </svg>
  </copy-clipboard>
 </i18n-message></h2>
<p>The content and information found on <a href="http://fews.net/" data-scope="external" rel="external noreferrer"><u>fews.net</u></a> and its subdomains is not official United States Government information and does not necessarily reflect the view of the United States Department of State or the United States Government.</p>
<p>Relevant data and information are organized and made available on the FEWS NET website and data center to promote their effective and wide-ranging use among FEWS NET’s global audience, including decision-makers responding to food security emergencies. FEWS NET works together with partners to ensure continued improvement in data availability, quality, accessibility, and use.&nbsp;</p>
<h2 id="Data-sources" style=""><span id="Dataandinformationuseandattributionpolicy-Datasources"></span><strong>Data sources</strong>
 <i18n-message i18nkey="clipboard.copyLink.label" attribute="description">
  <copy-clipboard class="anchor-copy-button" value="https://help.fews.net/fdp/data-and-information-use-and-attribution-policy#Data-sources">
   <svg role="img" aria-hidden="true" data-component="icon" class="icon icon-link copy-headline-link-icon">
    <use href="/__theme/b_8ca5a7d7-80f3-41c8-bea7-75cce14ad14f/scroll-sites-help-center-theme/57/icons.svg#link"></use>
   </svg>
  </copy-clipboard>
 </i18n-message></h2>
<p>Data and information available on the FEWS NET website and data platform are gathered from various sources. FEWS NET engages in regular data collection activities, compiles data from public online sources, and receives data through partnerships with United Nations partners, non-governmental organization partners, the FEWS NET Science Team, regional and intergovernmental organizations, and global institutions, among others.</p>
<p>FEWS NET publishes all data according to permissions provided by the original source institution, and all data sources are fully attributed. There is no attempt to otherwise duplicate the information or systems of other organizations.</p>

<h2 id="Attribution-policy" style=""><span id="Dataandinformationuseandattributionpolicy-Attributionpolicy"></span>Attribution policy
 <i18n-message i18nkey="clipboard.copyLink.label" attribute="description">
  <copy-clipboard class="anchor-copy-button" value="https://help.fews.net/fdp/data-and-information-use-and-attribution-policy#Attribution-policy">
   <svg role="img" aria-hidden="true" data-component="icon" class="icon icon-link copy-headline-link-icon">
    <use href="/__theme/b_8ca5a7d7-80f3-41c8-bea7-75cce14ad14f/scroll-sites-help-center-theme/57/icons.svg#link"></use>
   </svg>
  </copy-clipboard>
 </i18n-message></h2>
<p>Please note the following:</p>
<ul>
 <li>
  <p><strong>If using data provided through the </strong><a href="http://fews.net/" data-scope="external" rel="external noreferrer"><strong>FEWS NET Wesbite</strong></a><strong> in any way, direct or manipulated</strong>, you must include attribution for the data you use in the manner indicated in the metadata included with the data or documentation.</p>
 </li>
 <li>
  <p><strong>If generating a data file for public use or private use and presenting those data to another party</strong>, you agree to include the same acknowledgment requirement in any sub-licenses of the data that you grant, and a requirement that any sub-licensees do the same. You may meet this requirement by providing the uniform resource locator (URL) of these terms of use when it is developed.</p>
  <ul>
   <li>

   </li>
   <li>

   </li>
   <li>
    <p>Some data are provided by third parties, and may not be redistributed or reused without the consent of the original data provider, or may be subject to additional terms and conditions. Where applicable, these conditions are included in the dataset or indicator metadata.</p>
   </li>
  </ul>
 </li>
 <li>
  <p><strong>If using data collected by FEWS NET in a visual, graphic, map or infographic of your own</strong>, please attribute the source data clearly and prominently on the visual itself or within the key/legend and include a link to the <a href="http://fews.net/" data-scope="external" rel="external noreferrer">FEWS NET Website</a>.</p>
 </li>
 <li>
  <p><strong>If using third party data from other organizations hosted by the FEWS NET Data Warehouse or provided by the </strong><a href="http://fews.net/" data-scope="external" rel="external noreferrer"><strong>FEWS NET Website</strong></a>, please check the primary source’s website, and follow their policies on use and re-distribution of data. For instance, some data sources, such as the UN COMTRADE data, cannot be disseminated in any form without written permission of the United Nations Statistics Division. In the absence of specific guidance from the primary source any visual, graphic, map or infographic that you produce should attribute the primary source organization clearly and prominently on the visual itself or within the key/legend.</p>
 </li>
 <li>
  <p><strong>If you wish to reproduce or publish a graphic, graph, or map </strong><a href="http://fews.net/" data-scope="external" rel="external noreferrer"><strong>FEWS NET</strong></a><strong> has already published</strong> (rather than creating an original image using raw data) please cite the graphic, graph or map, including the author, using the following sample format [1]:</p>
