            <h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This data consists of: money and lending; monetary financial institutions' balance sheets; further analyses of deposits and lending; external business of banks operating in the UK, public sector debt and the money markets (including gilt repo and stock lending); sterling commercial paper, other debt securities, capital issues; financial derivatives and interest and exchange rates.</p>

<h3><strong style="font-size: 1.278em;">2026/2027 release dates</strong></h3>
<ul>
    <li>29 October 2026 (September 2026 data)</li>
