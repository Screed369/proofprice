<h2>Medicinal product price lists</h2><p>The prices of medicinal products that, in accordance with the marketing authorisation for the Republic of Croatia, are classified as prescription medicinal products with regard to the method of dispensing and the medicinal products that the marketing authorisation holder intends to market in the Republic of Croatia, are determined by HALMED in accordance with the Medicinal Products Act (Official Gazette, No. <a class="lnk_body" href="https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2013_06_76_1522.html" target="_blank">76/13</a>, <a class="lnk_body" href="https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2014_07_90_1809.html" target="_blank">90/14</a> and <a class="lnk_body" href="https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2018_11_100_1931.html" target="_blank">100/18</a>) and the Ordinance on the criteria for determining the highest permitted wholesale price of a medicinal product and the exceptional increase in the maximum permitted wholesale price of a medicinal product and the annually calculated price of a medicinal product (Official Gazette, No. <a class="lnk_body" href="https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2019_04_33_705.html" target="_blank">33/19</a>, <a class="lnk_body" href="https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2023_07_72_1191.html" target="_blank">72/23</a>).</p>
<p>On its website, HALMED regularly publishes the lists of medicinal products with a set highest permitted wholesale price as well as the list of medicinal products with the exceptional increase in the maximum permitted wholesale price.</p>
<p>The lists of published medicinal product prices in question are available in the spreadsheets below and are for reference purposes.</p>
