            <div class="article__head__date bg--orange">2026 REGULATED RATES</div>

        <div class="article__body">

            <p>Consumers who don't sign a contract for natural gas or electricity are served by a <a href="/utility-choices/retailers-and-distributors/#Regulated-Rate" title="Retailers and Distributors" data-anchor="#Regulated-Rate">default retailer</a>.</p>
<p>The default rates are reviewed and approved by the <a rel="noopener" href="https://www.auc.ab.ca/" target="_blank">Alberta Utilities Commission (AUC)</a>. The following tables show the approved default electricity and natural gas rates for residential and small business consumers across Alberta.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<h2>Electricity</h2>
<p>The&nbsp;<a href="/utility-choices/current-rates-and-pricing/rate-of-last-resort/" title="Rate of Last Resort">Rate of Last Resort (RoLR)</a> is the default rate for electricity. This was formerly known as the Regulated Rate Option (RRO). The RoLR will be in place until December 31, 2026.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: left;">These rates do not include billing and delivery costs. Compare retailer plans and check distributor fees in the <a href="/cost-comparison-tool/" title="Cost Comparison Tool">Cost Comparison Tool</a>.</p>
