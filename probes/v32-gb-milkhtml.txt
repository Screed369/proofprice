<div class="gem-c-heading govuk-!-margin-bottom-4">

  <h2 class="gem-c-heading__text govuk-heading-m">
    Details
</h2></div>

        <div data-module="govspeak" class="gem-c-govspeak gem-c-govspeak--direction-ltr govuk-!-margin-bottom-0">
    
          <div class="govspeak"><p>Most milk produced in the UK is bought by dairies (registered ‘milk purchasers’) for processing. Defra runs a monthly survey in England and Wales to collect information on the volume, value and protein content of milk purchased from farms. Similar surveys are run in Scotland and Northern Ireland. Additional information is collected by the Rural Payments Agency (RPA) on the protein and butterfat content of the milk. The UK average farm-gate milk price, protein content and butterfat content is then calculated.</p>

<h2 id="user-engagement">User Engagement</h2>

<p>Data from the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/milk-prices-and-composition">milk production and composition</a> statistics are an invaluable evidence base for policy makers, academics and researchers. The data is also heavily relied upon by the UK dairy sector, as well as the AHDB. The <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/milk-prices-and-composition">milk production and composition</a> data is used to understand the current state of the UK dairy sector, predict supply shortages and forecast future milk prices.</p>

<p>As part of our ongoing commitment to compliance with the <a rel="external" href="https://code.statisticsauthority.gov.uk/">Code of Practice for Official Statistics</a> we wish to strengthen our engagement with users of <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/milk-prices-and-composition">milk production and composition</a> data and better understand the use made of them and the types of decisions that they inform. Consequently, we invite users to register as a user of the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/milk-prices-and-composition">milk production and composition</a> data, so that we can retain your details and inform you of any new releases and provide you with the opportunity to take part in user engagement activities that we may run.  If you would like to <a rel="external" href="https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=UCQKdycCYkyQx044U38RAvMEgOxhWuZCpr9kfPf834FUNFM5NlZZUERSMVBENExKU09VWE9aRjhBWi4u">register as a user</a> of the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/milk-prices-and-composition">milk production and composition</a> data, please provide your details in the attached <a rel="external" href="https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=UCQKdycCYkyQx044U38RAvMEgOxhWuZCpr9kfPf834FUNFM5NlZZUERSMVBENExKU09VWE9aRjhBWi4u">form</a>.</p>
