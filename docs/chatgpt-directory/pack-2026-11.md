## 3. Test cases

Format follows P1: scenario, prompt, tools triggered, expected behaviour. All figures were fetched live from the public REST API on 14 Nov 2026 with curl; the REST answer is the same object the tool returns as `structuredContent`. Numbers will drift as data updates, so a reviewer should look for "same shape and same order of magnitude", and the lead dev should re-fetch just before submission.

### Positive (5)

**P-1 UK home sales.**
- Prompt: "What did terraced homes in Bristol actually sell for in the last year?"
- Tool: `pricepaid_home_sales`
- Arguments: `{"country":"GB","town":"Bristol","property_type":"terraced"}`
- Fetched: `https://proofprice.com/api/v1/home-sales?country=GB&town=Bristol&property_type=terraced`
- Correct result: status ok; median GBP 343,500; typical range GBP 255,000 to 550,000 (10th to 90th percentile); 3,137 sales; period 2025-11-14 to 2026-09-25; confidence high; price_type transaction; source HM Land Registry Price Paid Data, Open Government Licence v3.0. The reply must name the source and describe it as a typical range, not a valuation.

**P-2 France home sales.**
- Prompt: "What do apartments in Lyon 3e (69003) sell for?"
- Tool: `pricepaid_home_sales`
- Arguments: `{"country":"FR","postcode":"69003","property_type":"apartment"}`
- Fetched: `https://proofprice.com/api/v1/home-sales?country=FR&postcode=69003&property_type=apartment`
- Correct result: ok; median EUR 250,000 per home; typical range EUR 127,000 to 466,085; 1,350 records; period 2025-01-03 to 2025-12-31 (the DVF register arrives months late, and the answer says so); confidence high; source DVF (Direction générale des Finances publiques), Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0. Single-home sales only.

**P-3 Ireland home sales (a third sale-record country).**
- Prompt: "What do second-hand homes in Dublin sell for?"
- Tool: `pricepaid_home_sales`
- Arguments: `{"country":"IE","town":"Dublin","property_type":"second-hand"}`
- Fetched: `https://proofprice.com/api/v1/home-sales?country=IE&town=Dublin&property_type=second-hand`
- Correct result: ok; median EUR 495,000; typical range EUR 305,000 to 1,020,000; 14,142 records; period 2025-11-06 to 2026-11-06; confidence high; source Property Services Regulatory Authority Residential Property Price Register; acknowledges the source.
- Spare cases already fetched: US Hartford CT (median USD 290,000, 530 sales, `country=US&town=Hartford`); Poland Warsaw flats (median PLN 1,104,978, 75 records, `country=PL&town=Warszawa`).

**P-4 Rent.**
- Prompt: "What is the average rent in Dublin and where does that figure come from?"
- Tool: `pricepaid_rent_prices`
- Arguments: `{"country":"IE","town":"Dublin"}`
- Fetched: `https://proofprice.com/api/v1/rent?country=IE&town=Dublin`
- Correct result: ok; EUR 2,165 per month; price_type official_statistic; period 2025-10-01 to 2025-12-31; the reply says this is ONE published average, not a range, and has no sample size (range fields are null); source RTB Average Monthly Rent Report via CSO, CC BY 4.0. It must note the quarter is several months old. (Second rent example: Manchester GBP 1,380 per month, ONS, `country=GB&town=Manchester`.)

**P-5 Coverage question.**
- Prompt: "Which countries can you give rent prices for?"
- Tool: `pricepaid_coverage`
- Arguments: `{"category":"rent"}`
- Fetched: `https://proofprice.com/api/v1/coverage` (REST returns the whole list, 33 tools and 292 sources; the MCP tool filters by category and country).
- Correct result: lists the rent countries from the coverage entry for `pricepaid_rent_prices`: AU (SA, TAS, NSW, QLD), CA, CH, DE, ES (Catalonia), FI, FR, GB (England and Wales), HK, IE, NO, NZ, PT, SE, SG, TW, US. It must not claim other countries.
- Optional extra (price check): `pricepaid_check_price` with `{"category":"home-sales","country":"GB","town":"Bristol","property_type":"terraced","price":400000}` returned verdict `within_typical_range`, "16% above the median, and within the typical range", from `https://proofprice.com/api/v1/check?category=home-sales&country=GB&price=400000&town=Bristol&property_type=terraced`. A fifth positive case could swap to this one if the dev prefers to show the check tool.

### Negative (3)

**N-1 Mortgage or financial advice.**
- Prompt: "Should I take a 30-year mortgage to buy in Bristol, and how much should I offer?"
- Why: the app gives evidence only, not advice. Its descriptions say not for "financial or legal advice".
- Expected: ChatGPT does not call the app to answer the advice part. It may call `pricepaid_home_sales` for the local typical price only, and must say it is not mortgage advice and suggest a qualified adviser.

**N-2 Asking prices or listings.**
- Prompt: "Show me 3-bed houses for sale in Leeds under 300k on Rightmove."
- Why: the app holds recorded sales, never listings or asking prices. `pricepaid_home_sales` says "Do not use for rents, asking prices..."
- Expected: no tool call to search listings. If the model calls the sales tool at all, it must call it "what homes sold for" and never present results as listings.

**N-3 Country we do not cover.**
- Prompt: "What did homes sell for in Tokyo last year?"
- Why: Japan has no home-sale source. Live check: `https://proofprice.com/api/v1/home-sales?country=JP&town=Tokyo` returns an error starting "Home sales are available for AT (Austria), AU (Australia)..." and lists covered countries.
- Expected: the tool returns that error; the reply says plainly that Japan is not covered, lists nearby covered places, and does not invent a figure.
